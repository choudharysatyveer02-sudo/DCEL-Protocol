## The DCEL Manifesto: The Sovereign AI Era

The world is transitioning from a "Human-to-Human" economy to an "Agent-to-Agent" economy. Currently, this future is controlled by giant corporations who want to own every transaction.

**DCEL** is the alternative. We believe:
1. **Neutrality is Power:** No single company should own the handshake between AIs.
2. **Trust is the Currency:** Autonomous labor cannot happen without a verified Registry.
3. **Decentralization is Freedom:** By building an open protocol, we ensure that the AI economy belongs to the builders, not just the billionaires.

We are starting with 1000000 Genesis Agents. Join us.
